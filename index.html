About Angus News

Angus News is an independent local news outlet dedicated to reporting on stories that matter to our community. We focus on local governance, community events, and the voices of Angus and Simcoe County.

Our mission is to provide accurate, timely, and independent journalism that serves the public interest and strengthens community discourse.
